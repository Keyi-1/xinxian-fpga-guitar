idle threshold 64. Slide duration 960 samples. PA remains on after reset.

Anti-beat changes and evidence
-----------------------------
BOARD_ANTI_BEAT_ENABLE=1 by default in the new MAIN and FULL paths.
Pitch-aware tie choice for third voice; existing active voices keep their side.
At most two active voices per channel; first pair separated as before.
For a same-channel non-unison gap <80 Hz, the older voice gain moves toward
128/256; new voice stays at full gain. Gain step=2/256 per 48 kHz sample.
Mix headroom /2 retained. No retuning or change to per-voice synthesis.
Set BOARD_ANTI_BEAT_ENABLE=0 and rebuild to restore baseline mixing.
The range/gain are engineering choices, not a universal perceptual model.
See docs/ANTI_BEAT_AUDIO.md and antibeat_pcm_comparison.txt for limits.
Actual RTL A/B: 96,000 PCM16 stereo frames per variant, repeated 3/4-note
chords at F#4/E4/D#4/A#3, same velocity/plucks. Per-voice PCM, sample cadence
and lifecycle asserted identical. No separate WAV loudness normalization.
Constructed 3-note example normalized envelope depth: 0.838158 -> 0.489251.
These numbers do not measure perceived roughness or physical speaker output.
WAV/plot: build/antibeat/, regenerated with tools/generate_antibeat_preview.py.
