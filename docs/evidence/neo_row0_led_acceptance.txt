1. Acceptance scope

This evidence records ROW0 single-group preliminary hardware acceptance only.
ROW0 is tied directly to ground, ROW1 through ROW3 are disconnected, all eight
columns are FPGA inputs, and eight temporary LEDs show the debounced states.
There is no row scanning in this build.

The result proves only:
- all eight ROW0 contacts can be detected;
- COL0 through COL7 and LED0 through LED7 mapping is correct for this setup;
- the two-stage input synchronizer, approximately 10 ms debounce and reset
  behavior work;
- the listed PMOD physical connections are usable.

It does not prove ROW1 through ROW3, full 4x8 scanning, high impedance on
unselected rows, simultaneous four-string input, highest-fret priority,
multi-row diode ghost prevention, or integration with fret_note_event, glide
and voice control.

8. Next-stage plan

Stage A - remaining fixed-ground row checks:
- power off before every wiring change;
- keep the same eight COL inputs and eight LED outputs;
- move only the ground jumper from ROW0 to ROW1, then ROW2, then ROW3;
- keep all other rows disconnected and record all eight contacts for each row.

Stage B - full 4x8 scan:
- remove the temporary PMOD0 LED connections;
- restore the reviewed 4 ROW + 8 COL allocation in the board matrix document;
- keep CPU pins as regular I/O for P19/R19/T21/U21;
- instantiate the existing matrix_scanner, with active row low and unselected
  rows high impedance;
- observe row select, synchronized columns and stable matrix through GAO/JTAG;
- test single contacts, same-row multiple contacts, cross-row contacts and
  rectangular combinations that exercise the per-contact diodes.

Stage C - control-chain integration:
- verify highest-fret priority and open-string fallback in fret_note_event;
- verify matrix-to-four_string_voice_control integration;
- verify glide without Attack retrigger and final-fret release behavior;
- only then combine the matrix with the already verified audio path.
