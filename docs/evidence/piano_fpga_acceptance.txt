REPRODUCED AND FIXED SYNTHESIS DISCREPANCY
The former netlist differed in 476/792 serialized DAC frames from RTL.
The signed envelope interpolation DSP pre-adder narrowed env_a to 14 bits
and extended its upper input with ones. Subtracting this operand introduced
an extra +16384 into b-a. The 9-bit fraction then created a repeating
93.75-Hz amplitude ramp that did not exist in RTL or its preview WAVs.
Use registered unsigned products a*(512-fraction) and b*fraction instead,
then add and shift by 9. Correct sample-domain floor rounding is unchanged.
The original independent oracle and preview WAV hashes still match.

whether to retain this experimental default or program Flash.
