PROBLEM AND CHANGE
User hardware feedback: V1 lacks rock character and has a long dry tail even
with sustain disabled. V1 root/mid envelope taus 1.8/1.44 s retained nearly all
energy during the first half second. This was an unsuitable default dry pluck.
V2 prepares a single-note asymmetric overdrive waveform OFFLINE, projects it
into at most 32 harmonics, and renders those harmonics on FPGA. Excitation:
72% sine root + 28% measured-reference waveform; tanh drive 5, asymmetry .12;
remove DC; smooth rolloff near harmonic order 12; harmonic floor -60 dB.
13 nonzero coefficients; root is strongest; gain Q15 sum 32760. Coefficient
and phase tables are generated together, not only by increasing magnitudes.
Runtime has no new post-chord nonlinear clipping, PCM loop or broadband noise.
Maximum modal sum bound remains 16380 PCM16 units, prior two timbres unchanged.

Root/mid/high taus now .16/.128/.08 s. Pick attack 32 samples = .667 ms.
Envelope has a .008 s pick shoulder; final dry taper .4-.5 s; zero after .5 s.
Shared 2048 x 12-bit envelope ROM, 64-sample virtual steps, saturated addresses.
Sustain preserves the first 2048 samples, then advances age every 4 samples.
Same single unreleased C4 is zero after approximately 1.872 s with sustain.
Chorus, light room, fifth harmony, three keys, frequency table and CST unchanged.
